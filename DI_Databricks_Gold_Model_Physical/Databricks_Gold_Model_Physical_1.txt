# Databricks Gold Model Physical

## 1. Overview
This document translates the provided Gold logical model and Silver physical model into an implementation-ready Databricks Gold physical data model for the Rental Revenue-to-Cash domain. The Gold layer is designed for dimensional, fact, and aggregated reporting use cases. All Silver business tables and columns are considered in the Gold design, id fields are added to Gold tables, Spark SQL incompatible constraints are excluded, and operational audit and error tables are included.

## 2. Input Analysis

| Input File | Purpose | Physical Modeling Impact |
|---|---|---|
| `DI_Databricks_Silver_Model_Physical/Databricks_Silver_Model_Physical_1.md` | Defines conformed Silver physical schema and mandatory Silver business columns | Ensures all Silver columns and required operational structures are represented or traceable in Gold physical design |
| `DI_Databricks_Gold_Model_Logical/Databricks_Gold_Model_Logical_1.md` | Defines Gold logical entities, dimensions, facts, aggregates, audit, and error structures | Drives Gold table inventory, business grain, metrics, and relationship mapping |

## 3. Gold Layer Physical Design Principles
- Gold tables are created using `CREATE TABLE IF NOT EXISTS` and `USING DELTA`.
- Gold layer includes dimension, fact, aggregate, error, and audit tables.
- Every Gold table includes an id field as required by the instruction.
- No primary keys, foreign keys, unique constraints, generated identity clauses, or Spark-incompatible constraints are included.
- Datatypes are aligned for Databricks Spark SQL compatibility.
- `VARCHAR`, `DATE`, `TIMESTAMP`, `INT`, `BIGINT`, and `DECIMAL` are used; `TEXT`, `DATETIME`, and identity syntax are avoided.
- Standard metadata columns are included in each table: `load_date`, `update_date`, and `source_system`.
- Additional lineage fields are included where useful for reconciliation with Silver.
- Partitioning is selected based on reporting date, snapshot date, status, or low-cardinality domains.
- Traditional indexes are not implemented in Spark SQL; Delta optimization and Z-Ordering recommendations are provided instead.

## 4. Identified Sources, Transformations, Joins, Aggregations, Filters, and Outputs

### 4.1 Source Tables from Silver
- `silver.si_rental_contracts`
- `silver.si_invoices`
- `silver.si_cash_receipts`
- `silver.si_customer_master`
- `silver.si_branch_employee`
- `silver.si_data_quality_errors`
- `silver.si_process_audit`

### 4.2 Gold Transformations
- Build conformed dimensions for date, region, branch, customer, sales representative, collector, equipment class, contract, and invoice.
- Derive aging buckets and days past due from invoice and payment timing logic.
- Aggregate invoice and contract measures into rental revenue metrics.
- Aggregate outstanding receivables into AR aging snapshots.
- Aggregate receipts and applications into cash application performance metrics.
- Derive customer exposure and limit utilization into credit risk facts.
- Roll up fact metrics into executive health score and billing performance aggregates.
- Capture Gold validation failures in a Gold error data table.
- Capture Gold pipeline execution events in a Gold audit table.

### 4.3 Expected Joins in Gold Processing

| Left Table | Join Field | Right Table | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| `silver.si_rental_contracts` | `customer_id` | `silver.si_customer_master.customer_id` | Build customer and contract context |
| `silver.si_rental_contracts` | `sales_rep_id` | `silver.si_branch_employee.employee_id` | Build sales rep assignment |
| `silver.si_rental_contracts` | `branch_code` | `silver.si_branch_employee.branch_code` | Build branch and regional context |
| `silver.si_invoices` | `contract_id` | `silver.si_rental_contracts.contract_id` | Associate invoices to contracts |
| `silver.si_invoices` | `customer_id` | `silver.si_customer_master.customer_id` | Associate invoices to customers |
| `silver.si_cash_receipts` | `invoice_id` | `silver.si_invoices.invoice_id` | Associate payments to invoices |
| `silver.si_cash_receipts` | `customer_id` | `silver.si_customer_master.customer_id` | Associate payments to customers |
| `gold.go_fct_rental_revenue` | `reporting_date` | `gold.go_dim_date.reporting_date` | Time conformance |
| `gold.go_fct_ar_aging` | `aging_bucket` | `gold.go_code_aging_bucket.aging_bucket_name` | Bucket conformance |

### 4.4 Aggregations
- Daily and period rental revenue, gross billings, credit memo effect, net billings, invoice count.
- Snapshot AR exposure, 90+ bucket concentration, DSO, best possible DSO, aging bucket balances.
- Payment collections, unapplied cash, average days to pay, on-time payment ratio, collection effectiveness.
- Customer-level credit utilization, over-limit exposure, open contract exposure.
- Executive score rollups across network, region, branch, sales rep, and collector levels.
- Billing performance rollups by region, branch, sales rep, and equipment class.

### 4.5 Filters and Data Quality Rules
- Exclude or route invalid mandatory records to Gold error table.
- Derive aging bucket only from approved values: `0-30`, `31-60`, `61-90`, `90+`.
- Prevent divide-by-zero during metric derivation.
- Flag records where payment exceeds invoice amount.
- Flag records where gross billings minus credit memo does not reconcile to net billings.
- Flag negative financial measures where business rules require non-negative values.

### 4.6 Output Format
- Delta tables in Databricks Gold schema.
- Text artifact stored in GitHub output folder.

## 7. Data Retention Policies

### 7.1 Retention Periods for the Gold Layer
| Table Category | Retention Period | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| Gold dimensions | 7 years | Supports historical governed analytics and finance reporting traceability |
| Gold facts | 7 years | Supports long-range trend analysis, financial comparisons, and compliance reporting |
| Gold aggregates | 5 years | Supports executive reporting while permitting recomputation from facts if needed |
| Gold error data | 2 years | Supports remediation analysis and validation history |
| Gold process audit | 2 years | Supports operational auditability and SLA investigation |

### 7.2 Archiving Strategies
- Archive partitions older than retention threshold to lower-cost cloud storage while preserving replay capability.
- Recompute aggregates from retained facts where business policy allows rather than preserving all pre-aggregated data indefinitely.
- Apply Delta `VACUUM` only in accordance with enterprise governance and log retention requirements.
- Preserve summarized audit and error trends before archival for operational dashboards.
- Use date-driven archival on `reporting_date`, `snapshot_date`, or metadata timestamps depending on table grain.

## 8. Databricks Spark SQL Limitations Considered
The following items are intentionally not included because they are incompatible with or not preferred for Databricks Spark SQL physical modeling:
- No primary key constraints.
- No foreign key constraints.
- No unique constraints.
- No check constraints enforced in DDL.
- No `GENERATED ALWAYS AS IDENTITY` syntax.
- No `TEXT` datatype.
- No `DATETIME` datatype.
- No traditional RDBMS index definitions.

Instead, Delta partitioning, `OPTIMIZE`, and Z-Ordering are used.

## 9. Assumptions and Design Decisions
1. The Silver physical model is treated as the physical source baseline, and Gold design preserves the relevant business attributes required for analytical consumption.
2. Additional descriptive columns such as `branch_code`, `customer_id`, `sales_rep_id`, and `collector_id` are retained in Gold physical tables to support traceability and dimensional joins.
3. Because the prompt requires id fields in the Gold physical model, surrogate-style id columns are added to every Gold table without using identity generation syntax.
4. Invoice matching status and days past due are treated as derivable Gold attributes from Silver invoice and receipt data.
5. Credit memo measures are modeled in Gold facts and aggregates even though not explicitly present in current Silver physical DDL, because they are required by the Gold logical model and may be derived from invoice type and billing adjustment logic.
6. Error and audit tables are implemented in the Gold layer separately from Silver, as explicitly required.
7. Branch and region naming attributes are assumed to be standardized during Gold conformance from employee and contract mappings.
8. `VARCHAR` is used in DDL text for compliance with the instruction even though Databricks typically treats string-compatible text as `STRING` internally.
9. Conceptual relationships are documented as business-key relationships only and not enforced through constraints.
