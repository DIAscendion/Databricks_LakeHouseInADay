ASCHEAVYRENTALS — MEDALLION ARCHITECTURE
TABLE SCHEMAS (BRONZE / SILVER / GOLD)
================================================================
Required columns for every table in the Bronze, Silver, and Gold layers.

1. BRONZE LAYER — raw ingestion
----------------------------------------------------------------

bronze.purchase_order
  Source: /Performance Standards/Purchase Order
  Grain: 1 row per PO line
  Columns:
    - po_number          Purchase order number
    - po_date            Date PO was issued
    - vendor_id          Vendor identifier
    - equipment_id       Equipment unit purchased
    - equipment_class    Equipment class/type
    - cost_amount        PO line cost amount
    - status             Open / Received
    - received_date      Date equipment was received

bronze.ar_by_sales_rep_weekly
  Source: /Finance/AR/AR Reports by Sales Rep - WEEKLY
  Grain: 1 row per sales rep per week
  Columns:
    - week_ending        Week-ending date
    - sales_rep_id       Sales rep identifier
    - region              Region name
    - revenue_amount     Revenue booked that week
    - contract_count     Contracts opened that week
    - ar_balance         Outstanding AR balance

bronze.ar_reports
  Source: /Finance/AR/AR Reports
  Grain: 1 row per invoice/AR transaction
  Columns:
    - invoice_id         Invoice identifier
    - customer_id        Customer identifier
    - invoice_date       Date invoiced
    - due_date           Payment due date
    - paid_date          Date paid (null if open)
    - invoice_amount     Total invoice amount
    - open_balance       Remaining unpaid balance
    - aging_bucket       Aging category (Current, 1-30, 31-60, 61-90+)

bronze.bulk_lot_report
  Source: /Fleet/Bulk Lot Report
  Grain: 1 row per equipment unit, as-of snapshot
  Columns:
    - equipment_id       Equipment unit identifier
    - equipment_class    Equipment class/type
    - segment            General Tool / Specialty
    - region              Region name
    - original_cost      Original cost (OEC)
    - acquisition_date   Date acquired
    - status             Owned / On-rent / Idle

bronze.equipment_disposals
  Source: /Miscellaneous/Equipment Disposals
  Grain: 1 row per disposal transaction
  Columns:
    - equipment_id       Equipment unit disposed
    - disposal_date      Date disposed
    - original_cost      Original cost of the unit
    - book_value         Net book value at disposal
    - sale_proceeds      Cash proceeds from sale
    - disposal_method    Auction / Wholesale / Retail sale

bronze.gl_detail_asset
  Source: /Finance/Fixed Assets/GL Detail - Asset
  Grain: 1 row per GL asset transaction
  Columns:
    - gl_line_id         GL line identifier
    - date               Transaction date
    - equipment_id       Related equipment unit
    - account            GL account name
    - transaction_type   Depreciation / Capex / Disposal / Refurb Capex
    - amount             Transaction amount

bronze.gl_detail_datalake
  Source: /Finance/Fixed Assets/GL Detail - Data Lake
  Grain: 1 row per GL line
  Columns:
    - gl_line_id         GL line identifier
    - date               Transaction date
    - account            GL account name
    - department         Owning department/cost center
    - amount             Transaction amount
    - pl_line_mapped     Target management P&L line

bronze.invoice_detail_master
  Source: /Sales/Invoices/Invoice Detail - Master Account
  Grain: 1 row per invoice line
  Columns:
    - invoice_line_id    Invoice line identifier
    - invoice_date       Date invoiced
    - customer_id        Customer identifier
    - revenue_type       owned_rental / re_rent / ancillary / used_sale / new_sale
    - segment            General Tool / Specialty (rental lines only)
    - region              Region name
    - rate               Daily/period rental rate (rental lines only)
    - time_on_rent_days  Days on rent (rental lines only)
    - amount             Line amount

bronze.rental_contracts_by_rep
  Source: /Sales/Rental Contracts Opened by Sales Rep
  Grain: 1 row per contract opened
  Columns:
    - contract_id            Contract identifier
    - open_date              Date contract opened
    - sales_rep_id           Sales rep identifier
    - equipment_class        Equipment class/type
    - rate_realization_pct   Rate realization vs list rate
    - time_on_rent_days      Time on rent for the contract
    - region                  Region name

bronze.ap_vendor_rpt
  Source: /Finance/AP/AP_VendorRpt
  Grain: 1 row per AP transaction
  Columns:
    - ap_id               AP transaction identifier
    - vendor_id           Vendor identifier
    - invoice_date        Vendor invoice date
    - amount              Transaction amount
    - category            Equipment purchase / Parts & maintenance / Freight & delivery
    - paid_flag           Paid / Open

2. SILVER LAYER — cleaned facts & dimensions
----------------------------------------------------------------

silver.dim_equipment
  Grain: 1 row per equipment unit
  Columns:
    - equipment_id        Equipment unit identifier (business key)
    - equipment_class     Equipment class/type
    - segment             General Tool / Specialty
    - acquisition_date    Date acquired
    - useful_life_years   Depreciable useful life

silver.dim_region
  Grain: 1 row per region
  Columns:
    - region_id           Region identifier (business key)
    - region_name         Region display name

silver.dim_date
  Grain: 1 row per calendar date (fiscal calendar)
  Columns:
    - calendar_date       Calendar date
    - fiscal_year         Fiscal year
    - fiscal_month        Fiscal month number
    - month_end_flag      True if this date is a month-end

silver.dim_sales_rep
  Grain: 1 row per sales rep
  Columns:
    - sales_rep_id        Sales rep identifier (business key)
    - region_id           Assigned region

silver.fact_fleet_oec_daily
  Grain: equipment_id x date
  Columns:
    - equipment_id        Equipment unit identifier
    - date                Snapshot date
    - segment             General Tool / Specialty
    - region_id           Region identifier
    - original_cost       Original cost (OEC) as of date
    - month_end_flag      True if date is a month-end snapshot

silver.fact_revenue_transactions
  Grain: invoice_line_id x date
  Columns:
    - invoice_line_id     Invoice line identifier
    - date                Transaction date
    - customer_id         Customer identifier
    - revenue_type        owned_rental / re_rent / ancillary / used_sale / new_sale
    - segment             General Tool / Specialty (rental lines only)
    - rate                Daily/period rental rate (rental lines only)
    - time_on_rent_days   Days on rent (rental lines only)
    - amount              Line amount
    - region_id           Region identifier

silver.fact_ar_transactions
  Grain: invoice_id x date
  Columns:
    - invoice_id          Invoice identifier
    - date                Payment/reference date
    - customer_id         Customer identifier
    - invoice_amount      Total invoice amount
    - open_balance        Remaining unpaid balance
    - aging_days          Days outstanding, recalculated consistently

silver.fact_disposals
  Grain: disposal_id
  Columns:
    - disposal_id         Disposal transaction identifier
    - equipment_id        Equipment unit disposed
    - date                Disposal date
    - original_cost       Original cost of the unit
    - book_value          Net book value at disposal
    - sale_proceeds       Cash proceeds from sale
    - gain_loss           sale_proceeds - book_value, validated against GL

silver.fact_purchase_orders
  Grain: po_line_id
  Columns:
    - po_line_id          PO line identifier
    - date                Date received/capitalized
    - equipment_id        Equipment unit purchased
    - cost_amount         PO line cost amount
    - capitalized_flag    True once received and capitalized

silver.fact_gl_costs
  Grain: gl_line_id x date
  Columns:
    - gl_line_id          GL line identifier
    - date                Transaction date
    - pl_line             Mapped management P&L line
    - amount              Transaction amount

3. GOLD LAYER — KPI-ready tables
----------------------------------------------------------------

gold.kpi_scorecard  (section: The reading)
  Grain: company x period
  Columns:
    - grain                             Aggregation grain (e.g. Company)
    - period                            Reporting period (e.g. TTM Aug-2026)
    - revenue_usd_mm                    Total revenue ($M)
    - revenue_growth_pct                Revenue growth % YoY
    - fleet_oec_growth_pct              Fleet OEC growth %
    - same_store_revenue_growth_pct     Same-store revenue growth %
    - fleet_productivity_usd_mm         Fleet productivity $ (rate+time+mix)
    - dollar_utilization_pct            Company dollar utilization %
    - dollar_utilization_chg_pt         Change vs prior year (pt)
    - ebitda_margin_pct                 EBITDA margin %
    - ebitda_usd_mm                     EBITDA $ level
    - ebitda_chg_usd_mm                 EBITDA $ change YoY
    - fcf_usd_mm                        Simplified free cash flow $
    - net_fleet_investment_usd_mm       Net fleet investment $
    - disposal_proceeds_pct_of_cost     Disposal proceeds as % of original cost

gold.revenue_bridge  (section 2)
  Grain: company x period, one row per bridge component
  Columns:
    - component            Bridge component name
    - amount_usd_mm        Component amount ($M)

gold.dollar_utilization_by_segment  (section 3)
  Grain: segment x period (+ company roll-up row)
  Columns:
    - segment                       General Tool / Specialty / Company
    - share_of_fleet_pct            Share of fleet OEC %
    - dollar_utilization_pct        Dollar utilization %
    - chg_vs_py_pt                  Change vs prior year (pt)
    - design_band_min_pct           Target band minimum %
    - design_band_max_pct           Target band maximum %
    - capital_stance                Fund / Replace only / Hold recommendation
    - owned_rental_revenue_usd_mm   Owned rental revenue ($M)
    - avg_oec_usd_mm                Average original cost ($M)

gold.regional_mix  (section 4)
  Grain: region x period (+ company roll-up row)
  Columns:
    - region                  Region name / Company
    - revenue_usd_mm          Revenue ($M)
    - revenue_share_pct       Share of company revenue %
    - ebitda_margin_pct       EBITDA margin %
    - dollar_utilization_pct  Dollar utilization %

gold.management_pl  (section 5)
  Grain: period (August) + TTM, one row per P&L line
  Columns:
    - line_item          P&L line name
    - august_usd_mm      Current month amount ($M)
    - ttm_usd_mm         Trailing 12-month amount ($M)
    - pct_of_revenue     % of total revenue

gold.oec_rollforward  (section 5a)
  Grain: period, one row per bridge component
  Columns:
    - component          Roll-forward component name
    - amount_usd_mm      Component amount ($M)

gold.decision_metrics  (section 6)
  Grain: company x period
  Columns:
    - grain                            Aggregation grain (e.g. Company)
    - period                           Reporting period
    - rate_realization_pct_py          Rate realization %, prior year
    - rate_realization_pct_cy          Rate realization %, current year
    - time_on_rent_giveback_usd_mm     Time-on-rent $ giveback
    - specialty_pct_of_oec             Specialty % of fleet OEC
    - ebitda_growth_pct                EBITDA growth %
    - revenue_growth_pct               Revenue growth %
    - cost_of_rentals_ex_dep_usd_mm    Cost of equipment rentals excl. depreciation $
    - maintenance_usd_mm               Maintenance $
    - maintenance_pct_of_oec           Maintenance as % of month-end OEC
    - disposal_hurdle_pct              Disposal proceeds % of original cost (hurdle)
    - gain_on_disposal_usd_mm          Gain on disposal $
    - book_value_usd_mm                Book value at disposal $
    - cash_tax_usd_mm                  Cash tax $
    - working_capital_delta_usd_mm     Working capital change $
