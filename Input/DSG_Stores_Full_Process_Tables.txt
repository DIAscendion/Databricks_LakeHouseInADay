12. DATA QUALITY RULES
- Store must exist before inventory, sales, labor and shipment records.
- Product must exist before inventory or sales records.
- available_qty <= on_hand_qty.
- Inventory quantities must be non-negative.
- Shipment IDs must be unique.
- Athlete IDs must be unique.
