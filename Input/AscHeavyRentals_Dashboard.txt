0. SCOPE & GENERAL PARAMETERS
----------------------------------------------------------------
- Reporting period: Trailing 12 months (TTM), period-end = August 31, 2026.
  Prior-period comparison = TTM ended August 31, 2025.
- Currency / unit: US$, displayed in millions, 1 decimal place.
- Audience: CEO, CFO, COO (executive package).
- Data grain needed: Company, Segment (General Tool / Specialty), and
  Region (Southeast / Midwest / West). Some figures also need a single
  "current month" (August) column alongside the TTM column.
- Core ratio used throughout: DOLLAR UTILIZATION =
  TTM owned rental revenue (base rent + shift premiums) ÷ average of the
  twelve month-end original-cost (OEC) balances for the same period.
  Must be computed as a ratio of summed numerator/denominator ("ratio of
  sums"), NOT as an average of period utilization percentages.
- Explicitly OUT of the dollar-utilization ratio: delivery, fuel,
  waiver, damage, and re-rent revenue.

7. METRIC DEFINITIONS (must be encoded exactly as business rules)
----------------------------------------------------------------
- Dollar utilization = TTM owned rental revenue (base rent + shift
  premiums only) ÷ average of the 12 month-end OEC balances.
  Computed as ratio-of-sums at every grain (segment, company); never
  as a simple average of period percentages.
- Fleet productivity = combined rate + time + mix effect on owned
  rental revenue, after removing the effect of fleet-size growth
  (i.e., revenue bridge subtotal, section 2).
- EBITDA = Operating income + Depreciation of rental equipment +
  Non-rental depreciation & amortization. No one-time/non-recurring
  adjustments are applied in this pack.
- Simplified free cash flow = EBITDA − Cash tax − Net fleet capex.
  Net fleet capex = Fleet additions − Disposal proceeds (cash-in).
  Capitalized refurbishment is part of the OEC bridge (section 5a)
  and explicitly excluded from the net fleet capex figure.
